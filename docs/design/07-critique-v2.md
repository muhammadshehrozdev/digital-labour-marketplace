# Design critique v2 (Batch 4, Part 2)

> Generated by `docs/tools/generate-critique.mjs` from `docs/tools/critique-data.mjs`. Do not edit by hand.

**What this is.** The team's feedback after Batch 3 was: *"The design is still nothing special. Improve
and refine it a lot."* Before changing anything, every one of the 28 screens from `npm run screenshots`
was looked at in light mode at 1280 px and 360 px (English) and scored from 1 to 10 on ten criteria.

**Honest limits.** The scores are Claude Code's own judgement of the screenshots against the rubric
below. They are not a user test and not an expert panel; the team should look at the before/after pairs
in `docs/design/screenshots/v3/` and disagree where they see it differently. "Motion" cannot be seen in
a still image: it is scored from how the screen behaves in the browser (hover, focus, transitions,
loading), with reduced motion respected. "Layout" is the layout at that width; in the 360 px row it is the
"mobile layout" criterion of the prompt.

## Rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Missing or broken (for example no imagery at all, a table cut off, text hard to read). |
| 4-5 | Present but generic or uneven: works, nobody would remember it. |
| 6-7 | Solid and consistent, but plain: a tidy student project. |
| 8 | Good: clear intent, few rough edges. |
| 9 | Professional: comparable in care to well-known products (Stripe, Linear, Airbnb, Apple, Material 3), without copying them. |
| 10 | Exceptional: nothing to improve at this size. |

| Criterion | What is judged |
|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Is there one clear first thing to look at, then a second, then the rest? One main action per screen? |
| Type | Scale, weights, line length, line height, tracking of display text, numbers, Urdu line height. |
| Spacing | An 8 px rhythm; related things close, unrelated things apart; no dead areas. |
| Colour | Emerald for trust and actions, saffron as one accent, calm neutrals; meaning never by colour alone. |
| Imagery | Illustrations that explain or welcome, in one style. No stock photos of people. |
| Depth | Layered surfaces, soft multi-layer shadows, fewer borders, tasteful texture. |
| Consistency | The same component looks and behaves the same everywhere. |
| Motion | Meaningful transitions and feedback; nothing moves with reduced motion. |
| Microcopy | Short, warm, specific words; errors that say what to do. |
| Layout | Use of the width: grids, alignment, nothing cut off, comfortable touch targets at 360 px. |

### The three biggest problems of each screen

**Language chooser** (`00-language-chooser`)

1. Three equal cards with nothing to look at first: no focal point besides the small logo.
2. No imagery: a dark gradient and text only.
3. On desktop the block floats in a very tall empty page; hints are tiny (12 px).

**Landing** (`01-landing`)

1. After the hero every section is the same grid of equal white cards: no rhythm, nothing memorable.
2. Logo and language switcher are repeated inside the hero, right under the header.
3. Trades are a list of small rows with icons: no illustration, no reason to hover or tap.

**Log in** (`02-login`)

1. A small card at the top of a large empty page; the footer looks heavier than the form.
2. The dark side panel is a list of three lines: no illustration, no depth.
3. Labels and helper text are 12-13 px; the field has no leading icon or state styling.

**Create account: role** (`03-register-roles`)

1. The two role cards are small and look like form fields, yet this is the main decision.
2. Same empty page and heavy footer as Log in.
3. The role art is tiny (40 px) and does not match any other drawing.

**Create account: form** (`04-register-form`)

1. Fields are stacked with uneven gaps; the phone field has no mask or example formatting.
2. Password rules are a grey line under the field: easy to miss.
3. Side panel repeats Log in without adding anything for a new user.

**Page not found** (`05-not-found`)

1. An icon, one line and a button in a wide empty card: it looks unfinished.
2. No illustration and no way forward except "home" (no search, no useful links).
3. Text is flat: "This page does not exist." does not help the visitor.

**Onboarding 1: About you** (`10-onboarding-1-about`)

1. A plain two-column form in a card: nothing tells the worker why each private field is asked.
2. No imagery at all on the first screen a new worker sees.
3. Input heights differ (WhatsApp and CNIC look taller than the rest); no masks.

**Onboarding 2: Your work** (`11-onboarding-2-work`)

1. The page title got a focus ring that looks like an input (the heading is focused after the step change).
2. Rates are plain number boxes with no "Rs" prefix or grouping.
3. Narrow card in a wide page; the right half of the screen is unused.

**Onboarding 3: Your skills** (`12-onboarding-3-skills`)

1. The most important step is a long stack of similar cards; the path "write, find, choose" is not visible.
2. Toasts ("Saved") sit on top of the form on phones and cover fields.
3. Matched words appear only as small chips under each skill, far from the text the worker wrote.

**Onboarding 4: Proof** (`13-onboarding-4-proof`)

1. Three radio cards, a drop zone and a form in one long column with equal weight.
2. The drop zone is a dashed box with a small icon; it does not say "take a clear photo of your certificate".
3. No preview step: no crop, rotate, progress or cancel.

**Onboarding 5: All set** (`14-onboarding-5-done`)

1. The celebration is a yellow strip with an emoji-sized icon; the moment does not feel earned.
2. The summary is a grey table of labels and values.
3. Next actions (add proof, view profile) are not offered as clear choices.

**Worker dashboard** (`20-worker-dashboard`)

1. Five full-width cards of equal weight: no bento, no "what should I do now?"
2. Completion is one ring with no tips; proof is three numbers in a row.
3. No imagery and a lot of empty card area on desktop.

**My profile** (`21-profile-view`)

1. The header card mixes name, chips, a switch and a button on one line; the name wraps badly.
2. Skills are thin rows with three small grey labels each: evidence strength is hard to compare.
3. Cards are separated only by 1 px borders; the page reads as a form, not a profile.

**My profile: customer view** (`22-profile-customer-view`)

1. The only sign of "customer view" is a thin blue note at the top.
2. Same weak skill rows as My profile.
3. The switch has no animation between the two views.

**Edit profile: About** (`23-edit-about`)

1. The side tabs are small text links; the active tab is a faint tint.
2. Private fields look exactly like public ones (only a grey hint tells them apart).
3. No autosave or draft: closing the tab loses typed text.

**Edit profile: Work and rates** (`24-edit-work`)

1. Rates have no currency prefix or thousands separator.
2. Long grey explanation line above the form at 12 px.
3. Save button floats at the bottom right with no "saved" state.

**Edit profile: Skills** (`25-edit-skills`)

1. The large empty text box dominates; the skills already on the profile come second.
2. Removing a skill is immediate and cannot be undone.
3. No suggestions of related skills from the same trade.

**Edit profile: Proof** (`26-edit-proof`)

1. Upload form and the list of proof have the same weight; the list is far below the fold.
2. Thumbnails are 40 px: the worker cannot check what was uploaded.
3. Status and strength are small grey words in a row.

**Edit profile: Work history** (`27-edit-history`)

1. A job is four boxes in a bordered box inside a card: three nested borders.
2. "Add a past job" is a full-width dashed line that looks disabled.
3. Empty page area below a short form.

**Edit profile: Language** (`28-edit-language`)

1. Three wide radio rows with one globe icon each: no preview of what changes.
2. Theme and text size are not here (they did not exist).
3. Large unused area on desktop.

**Find workers** (`30-customer-search`)

1. Result cards are small and dense: three tiny number columns, two status chips, skill chips.
2. Filters are a narrow column of form fields; applied filters are not visible as chips.
3. No sorting, saving or comparing; an empty result is a plain sentence.

**Worker public profile** (`31-worker-public-profile`)

1. The profile header is a wide white card with a 64 px avatar: no presence.
2. The note about contact details is the first thing on the page.
3. No actions for the customer (save, compare, share, print).

**Admin overview** (`40-admin-overview`)

1. Ten tiles with one number each, in a 3-column grid with an orphan tile on the last row.
2. No charts, no trend, no activity: the admin cannot see what changed.
3. Queues that need action look the same as plain counts.

**Admin identity review** (`41-admin-identity`)

1. The table is wider than its card at 1280 px: the last column is cut off.
2. Rows do not show how long a worker has been waiting.
3. The worker name wraps over four lines in a narrow column.

**Admin identity drawer** (`42-admin-identity-drawer`)

1. Details are a grey label/value list; the decision area starts below the fold on small laptops.
2. No keyboard shortcuts and no reason templates: every note is typed again.
3. The page behind is only dimmed: no sense of a layered surface.

**Admin evidence review** (`43-admin-evidence`)

1. Four filters in one row push the table down; the table again touches the card edge.
2. No thumbnail in the list: every item must be opened to see what it is.
3. No waiting time; "possible duplicate" is a dash or a word.

**Admin evidence drawer** (`44-admin-evidence-drawer`)

1. The confidence numbers are four small figures and a long grey formula line.
2. A possible duplicate cannot be compared with the earlier photo side by side.
3. Three buttons of similar weight; "Suspected fake" is as loud as "Approve".

**Admin audit log** (`45-admin-audit`)

1. Raw technical values (object ids, dotted action names) in narrow wrapping columns.
2. The table is cut off on the right at 1280 px.
3. No sign of whether the hash chain is intact; no export.

### The same problems, grouped (what the redesign must fix)

1. **Imagery is almost absent** (average 2.7): one hero drawing and icons in tiles. Needed: one in-house
   illustration system (trades, empty states, onboarding steps, 404).
2. **Everything is a white card with a 1 px border.** No layering, no texture, no elevation levels.
3. **Short pages float above a heavy footer** (login, 404, onboarding, admin): the width is not used.
4. **Equal weight everywhere:** grids of identical cards on the landing page, dashboard and admin overview.
5. **Small, grey supporting text** (12-13 px) carries important information (privacy, strength, rules).
6. **Tables are wider than their cards** on the admin pages at 1280 px.
7. **Little feedback:** no undo, no drafts, no progress, toasts that cover the form on phones.
