# Price pilot plan (Module 5) — a PLAN only, no results

The price-range simulation (`backend/evaluation/price-range/`) uses assumed prices. Real prices are needed before
any statement about real prices can be made. This is how the team can collect them. **Nothing here has been done
yet; this file contains no results.**

## Goal
1. Fill the admin price guide (the fallback) with ranges that have a written, checkable source.
2. Later, compare the platform's own range with those ranges once enough real, both-confirmed jobs exist.

## Who and how (with consent)
- 5 to 10 workers per trade in one or two areas the team knows (for example G-9 and Satellite Town), and a few
  customers. Ask in person; read the consent text aloud in Urdu (based on `docs/evaluation/consent-form.md`):
  what is asked, that answers are anonymous, that they may stop at any time.
- Ask for **recent** prices only (last 3 months): the work, whole job / day / hour, the amount, who bought the
  materials. No names, no phone numbers, no addresses are written down.
- Record the answers in a sheet with an id per answer, the date, the area and the trade (see
  `docs/evaluation/data-handling.md`: stored by the team, not in the app's database).

## Using the answers
- A price guide entry only from at least 5 answers by at least 3 different people, as the middle half (25th–75th
  percentile) — the same rule as the platform range.
- The `sourceNote` names the survey: for example "Team survey of 6 electricians in G-9, Nov 2026 (n = 6)".
- Archive an entry after 12 months, or when a newer survey exists.

## What will not be claimed
- The pilot gives local, small-sample ranges. It will not be reported as "the fair price" or as representative of a
  city. Any number quoted in a report gives n, the area and the month.
