# Google Form draft — "Describe a job you needed done at home" (Module 6)

The team builds the form **later, with the user**, from this draft. Nothing here is live yet.
The Urdu and Roman Urdu questions are drafts for team review (`docs/i18n/REVIEW-BATCH-8A.md`).

## Settings (Google Forms → Settings)

- **Collect email addresses: OFF** (no email, no name).
- **Require sign-in: OFF** ("Restrict to users in your organisation" off; "Limit to 1 response" off, because that
  needs a sign-in; a person may describe more than one job).
- **No file upload question** (a file upload forces a Google sign-in and could hold photos or voice).
- **Responses:** viewable only by the team (do not share the response sheet; do not publish the summary).
- **After submitting:** "Show link to submit another response" ON.
- Form description: the consent text of `consent-text.md` (the language the person chooses; all three versions
  can be one after the other).

## Questions, in this order

**1. Consent (required, one checkbox)**
- EN: I have read the text above and I agree that my description is used to test the program.
- UR: میں نے اوپر کی تحریر پڑھ لی ہے اور میں متفق ہوں کہ میری بات پروگرام جانچنے کے لیے استعمال ہو۔
- Roman: Main ne upar ki tehreer parh li hai aur main muttafiq hoon ke meri baat program jaanchne ke liye istemaal ho.

**2. Age (required, one checkbox)**
- EN: I am 18 years old or older.
- UR: میری عمر 18 سال یا اس سے زیادہ ہے۔
- Roman: Meri umar 18 saal ya us se zyada hai.

**3. The description (required, paragraph)**
- EN: Describe one job that you needed done at home, in your own words. Any language is fine. Please do not write
  your name, phone number, house number or exact address.
- UR: گھر کا کوئی ایک کام جو آپ کو کروانا پڑا، اپنے الفاظ میں بیان کریں۔ کوئی بھی زبان ٹھیک ہے۔ براہ کرم اپنا نام،
  فون نمبر، مکان نمبر یا پورا پتہ نہ لکھیں۔
- Roman: Ghar ka koi ek kaam jo aap ko karwana para, apne alfaaz mein bayan karein. Koi bhi zubaan theek hai. Meharbani
  karke apna naam, phone number, makan number ya poora pata na likhein.
- **No example sentence** under the question (examples would teach people our words; `COLLECTION-PLAN.md`).

**4. Code word for deletion (optional, short answer)**
- EN: Optional: choose a code word. If you send it to us later, we delete your description.
- UR: اختیاری: کوئی کوڈ ورڈ چنیں۔ اگر آپ بعد میں یہ ہمیں بھیجیں تو ہم آپ کی بات مٹا دیں گے۔
- Roman: Ikhtiyari: koi code word chunein. Agar aap baad mein yeh humein bhejein to hum aap ki baat mita denge.

**5. Another job? (text only, no answer)**
- EN: Do you want to describe another job? Submit this form, then open it again.
- UR: کیا آپ کوئی اور کام بیان کرنا چاہتے ہیں؟ یہ فارم جمع کریں، پھر دوبارہ کھولیں۔
- Roman: Kya aap koi aur kaam bayan karna chahte hain? Yeh form jama karein, phir dobara kholein.
