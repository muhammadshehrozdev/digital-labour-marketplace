# Consent text — job descriptions for a student project (Module 6)

**Final text, reviewed by the team with Batch 8B (24 Nov 2026).** It is the description of the Google Form
(`FORM-DRAFT.md`) and is read out for the paper form used in person (`COLLECTION-PLAN.md`). The same words in three
languages. Two placeholders stay until the team fills them in the Google Form: **[team email]** and **[last date]**.

Supervisor's guidance (24 Nov 2026, reported by the team; `docs/modules/MODULE-6.md` §11, D-091): no ethics approval is
needed for this collection; the form must say clearly that it collects descriptions for the project (it does, in the
first two sentences). The form shows **no example job** anywhere (examples would teach people our words).

## Form title

Describe a job you needed at home — student project / گھر کے کام کی تفصیل — طلبہ کا پراجیکٹ / Ghar ke kaam ki tafseel — talaba ka project

## English

We are final-year students of BS Software Engineering at SZABIST Islamabad (supervisor: Ms. Tooba Abdul Qudoos Khan).
We are collecting short job descriptions for our project. We use them only to test a program that reads how people
describe a job they need done at home and suggests the kind of worker and the details.

- Taking part is your choice. Only people aged 18 or older.
- Please do not write your name, phone number, CNIC, house number or exact address. You may write the city.
- Your words are used only for this university project. They are not sold and not shared outside our team and our
  supervisor. We may quote a short sentence in our report without anything that shows who you are.
- We do not collect your email address, and you do not need to sign in.
- To have your answer deleted: write a code word below, and send it to [team email] before [last date].

## Roman Urdu

Hum SZABIST Islamabad mein BS Software Engineering ke aakhri saal ke talib-e-ilm hain (nigran: Ms. Tooba Abdul Qudoos
Khan). Hum apne project ke liye ghar ke kaamon ki mukhtasar tafseel jama kar rahe hain. Yeh sirf aik program ko
jaanchne ke liye istemaal hogi jo parhta hai ke log ghar ke kisi kaam ko kaise bayan karte hain, aur batata hai ke kis
qisam ka karigar chahiye aur kaam ki tafseel kya hai.

- Hissa lena aap ki marzi hai. Sirf 18 saal ya us se zyada umar ke log.
- Barah-e-karam apna naam, phone number, CNIC, makan number ya poora pata na likhein. Shehar likh sakte hain.
- Aap ke alfaaz sirf is university project ke liye istemaal honge. Yeh na bechay jayenge aur na hamari team aur nigran
  ke ilawa kisi ko diye jayenge. Hum apni report mein koi chhota jumla likh sakte hain, magar aisi koi baat nahi jis se
  aap ki pehchaan ho.
- Hum aap ka email jama nahi karte, aur sign in ki zaroorat nahi.
- Apna jawab hazf karwane ke liye: neeche aik code word likhein aur [aakhri tareekh] se pehle [team ka email] par bhej
  dein.
