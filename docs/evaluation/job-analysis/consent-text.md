## English (simple)

**Project:** Digital Labour Marketplace — Final Year Project, BS Software Engineering, SZABIST Islamabad.
Supervisor: Ms. Tooba Abdul Qudoos Khan.

We are students. We are testing a computer program that reads how a person describes a job they need done at home
(for example a repair, cleaning or moving) and suggests the kind of worker and the details of the job.

**What we ask:** describe **one** job that you needed done at home, in your own words, in any language (English, Urdu,
Roman Urdu or mixed). A few sentences are enough. You may fill the form again for another job.

- Taking part is **your choice**. You can say no, or stop at any time, with no problem for you.
- Only people who are **18 years or older** may take part.
- **Do not write** your name, phone number, CNIC, house number or exact address. Write the city if you like, but not
  the street or house.
- Your words are used **only** to test our program for our university project. They are **not sold** and **not
  shared** outside our team and our supervisor.
- We may quote a short sentence in our report **without** anything that shows who you are.
- **Deletion:** you may choose a code word in the form. Until **[date the test set is frozen]** you can send us that
  code word and we will delete your description. Contact: **[team email]**.
- We do not collect your email address, and you do not need to sign in.

☐ I have read this and I agree. ☐ I am 18 or older.
